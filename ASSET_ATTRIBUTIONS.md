# Brand assets

Editor logos identify the applications offered by the Open in selector. They are sourced from the respective official brand resources and retain their original artwork.

| Asset | Official source |
| --- | --- |
| `frontend/public/editors/vscode.svg` | [Visual Studio Code brand resources](https://code.visualstudio.com/brand), `visual-studio-code-icons/vscode.svg` in the official icon archive |
| `frontend/public/editors/phpstorm.svg` | [JetBrains brand resources](https://www.jetbrains.com/company/brand/), [PhpStorm SVG](https://resources.jetbrains.com/storage/logos/web/phpstorm/phpstorm.svg) |
| `frontend/public/editors/zed.png` | [Zed brand resources](https://zed.dev/brand), stable application icon |
| `frontend/public/integrations/jira.svg` | [Atlassian logo library](https://atlassian.design/resources/logo-library), original Jira icon |

Visual Studio Code, PhpStorm, Zed, GitHub, and Jira names and marks belong to their respective owners. Their appearance identifies supported editors or integrations and does not imply endorsement of M.I.E.R.

## Language-server catalog

The bundled logos in `frontend/public/language-servers` identify the corresponding language or tool. They load locally and work offline. The exact upstream files and immutable revisions are recorded in [sources.json](frontend/public/language-servers/sources.json).

- Language and tool icons come from [vscode-icons](https://github.com/vscode-icons/vscode-icons/tree/cd934f2d451fafce2ff9ad294f4e5767c5ca4e2f/icons), Copyright The vscode-icons Contributors, under the [MIT license](frontend/public/language-servers/VSCODE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt).
- The GitHub Actions and Semantic Web (Turtle/RDF) marks come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), under [CC0](frontend/public/language-servers/SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt).
- The unchanged jq icon comes from [jq's documentation](https://github.com/jqlang/jq/blob/6b53f8031161db96387b64b5f412845cd23491f6/docs/public/icon.svg), copyright Stephen Dolan and contributors, under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); see [upstream licensing](frontend/public/language-servers/JQ-COPYING.txt).

Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners and do not imply endorsement. These third-party asset licenses do not license M.I.E.R.'s source code.
